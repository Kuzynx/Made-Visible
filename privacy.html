Made Visible (“Made Visible,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when visiting our website, contacting us, or using our services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your information.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Business or company name
  • Website URL
  • Information submitted through contact forms
  • Information you provide when requesting a consultation, quote, or service
  • Communications between you and Made Visible

We may also automatically collect certain information when you visit our website, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited
  • Time spent on the website
  • Referring website or source
  • General geographic location
  • Website interaction and usage data

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to inquiries and contact requests
  • Provide information about our marketing services
  • Schedule consultations or meetings
  • Provide quotes, proposals, and services
  • Communicate with current and prospective clients
  • Improve our website, marketing, and services
  • Analyze website traffic and visitor behavior
  • Measure advertising and marketing performance
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, or security issues
  • Comply with applicable laws and legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information for money.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies, pixels, analytics tools, and similar technologies to understand how visitors interact with our website and to improve our marketing.

These technologies may collect information such as your device information, IP address, pages visited, traffic source, and interactions with our website.

We may use third-party tools and platforms such as Google Analytics, Google Ads, Meta/Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, or similar marketing and analytics services.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain website features.

4. Advertising and Analytics

Made Visible may use third-party advertising and analytics platforms to measure website performance, understand visitor behavior, and deliver relevant advertising.

These third parties may use cookies, tracking pixels, or similar technologies in accordance with their own privacy policies.

We do not control how third-party platforms independently collect or process information.

5. How We Share Information

We may share information with trusted third parties when necessary to operate our business, including:

  • Website hosting providers
  • Analytics providers
  • Advertising platforms
  • Email and communication providers
  • Customer relationship management platforms
  • Payment processors
  • Contractors or service providers assisting Made Visible
  • Legal or regulatory authorities when required by law

We only share information when reasonably necessary to operate our website, provide our services, conduct marketing activities, protect our rights, or comply with legal obligations.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, loss, misuse, or alteration.

However, no website, internet transmission, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, maintain business records, comply with legal requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we have collected about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of certain personal information
  • Request information about how your data is used
  • Opt out of certain marketing communications
  • Withdraw consent where applicable
  • Request that certain uses or disclosures of your personal information be limited

To make a privacy request, contact us at madevisiblemv@gmail.com.

We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests.

9. California Privacy Rights

California residents may have additional rights under applicable California privacy laws, including the California Consumer Privacy Act (“CCPA”) and California Privacy Rights Act (“CPRA”).

Depending on applicable legal requirements, California residents may have the right to request access, correction, or deletion of certain personal information and information regarding how personal information is collected, used, disclosed, shared, or sold.

Made Visible does not sell personal information for monetary compensation.

If our use of advertising technologies constitutes “sharing” of personal information under applicable California law, eligible California residents may have the right to opt out of such sharing.

Privacy requests may be submitted to madevisiblemv@gmail.com.

If you provide your email address or other contact information, we may communicate with you regarding inquiries, services, proposals, updates, or marketing.
