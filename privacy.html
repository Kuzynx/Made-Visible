Privacy policy.

Plain-English, because that’s how we work.

What we collect. When you submit the inquiry form, we receive the details you enter: your name, business name, email, phone, website, budget range and message. That’s it — we don’t collect anything you don’t type.

How it’s delivered. Form submissions are relayed to our inbox by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-forwarding service. Your submission passes through their systems in order to reach us.

What we do with it. We use your details to respond to your inquiry and discuss working together. We don’t sell, rent or share your information with anyone else, and we don’t add you to mailing lists you didn’t ask for.

Cookies & tracking. This site sets no advertising or analytics cookies. The only thing stored in your browser is a small session flag that prevents the intro animation from replaying as you browse — it identifies nothing about you and disappears when you close the tab.

Third-party requests. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google’s servers when a page loads. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare, which process requests the way any web host does.

Your choices. Want the details you sent us corrected or deleted? Email madevisiblemv@gmail.com and we’ll take care of it.
