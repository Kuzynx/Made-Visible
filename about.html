About Made Visible.

Being online isn’t enough. You need to be visible.

Who we are

Built for businesses that deserve to be seen.

Made Visible exists because great businesses go unnoticed every day — not for lack of quality, but for lack of presence. We started this agency to close that gap: to give businesses of any industry the strategy, design and content their work deserves.

We are strategists, designers, photographers, filmmakers and marketers under one roof — a full-service team with a single obsession: making your business seen, found, recognized and remembered.

The team

The people who make you visible.

Founder & CEO

Matthew Avakian

A young entrepreneur with 7 years in social media marketing and content creation. After working with brands across multiple industries and generating millions of views for businesses, Matthew created Made Visible to help businesses and brands stand out, adapt and grow in today’s constantly evolving marketing world.
